# <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/umenzi/umenzi/main/wave.gif" width="30px"> Hi there

My name is Samith S Palan, a student currently pursuing my B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering at Sahyadri College of Engineering and Management (Class of 2027).

Previously, I have actively participated in national-level hackathons, building everything from functional prototypes at St. Aloysius College to secure AI agents at the Authorized to Act hackathon. I've also served as a Technical Mentor for the Synergia Event, guiding students on science projects.

💻 My core interests lie within the fields of **Full-Stack Web Development** (MERN), **Data Engineering** (Apache Spark, Airflow, Delta Lake), and **Agentic AI**.

🚀 I am currently building scalable applications (like *HostelOps* and *Aarambh*), preparing for frontend and backend roles, and exploring browser extension development!
